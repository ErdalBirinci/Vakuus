Service 06 · EU regulation readiness

NIS2, EU AI Act, SOC 2 and GDPR in one programme

European requirements rarely arrive one at a time. We map which obligations actually apply to your organisation, then build a single control set that satisfies them together instead of running four parallel projects.

  • NIS2 readiness — governance, risk management, incident handling, supply chain security and business continuity assessed against the directive's expectations.
  • EU AI Act alignment — inventory of AI systems, risk classification, data governance and transparency controls mapped to ISO/IEC 42001.
  • SOC 2 readiness — Trust Services Criteria mapped onto your existing controls so a US customer's questionnaire closes quickly.
  • GDPR accountability — records of processing, DPIA triggers, retention, processor agreements and privacy-by-design review.
