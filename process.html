Phase by phase

What happens, what you get, what we need

01Week 1

Discovery & scoping workshop

Kickoff

We start with the reason you are here: a customer requirement, a tender, an investor request, a US customer's SOC 2 questionnaire or an EU obligation. Then we define the boundary — legal entities, locations, services, people and technology that fall inside the certificate's scope — and choose the right standard.

Scope statement Standard selection Stakeholder map Programme plan
02Week 2–3

Clause-by-clause gap assessment

Assessment

We interview your team, inspect your Microsoft 365 / cloud configuration, review existing documents and test all 93 Annex A controls plus Clauses 4–10. Each gap is graded and assigned an owner. The result is a remediation plan with sequence and dates — the document that shows what the project really takes in time.

Gap register Severity & owner per finding Remediation roadmap
03Week 4

Risk assessment & Statement of Applicability

Core

A workshop with your leadership: what do we protect, from what threats, how likely and how damaging, what do we do about it. Inherent and residual risk are recorded, treatment selected, owners named. The Statement of Applicability follows directly from those decisions — every control justified in writing.

Asset register Risk register Treatment plan SoA
04Week 5–10

Control implementation & documentation

Build

The longest phase, and where most projects lose momentum. We write the policies and procedures, configure the controls in your live environment, set up supplier reviews, access recertification, logging and monitoring baselines, secure development practices and the awareness programme — filing evidence as each control goes live.

Policy & procedure set Configured controls Evidence log Awareness training
05Week 11–12

Internal audit & management review

Assurance

An independent internal audit performed the way a certification body would run it — sampling, interviews, objective evidence. Findings are graded, root causes analysed and corrective actions closed. Then a management review turns the results into decisions with owners, giving Clause 9.2 and 9.3 their evidence.

Audit plan & report Corrective actions Management review minutes
06Week 13–18

Stage 1, Stage 2 & certification decision

Audit

Stage 1 reviews documentation and scope; Stage 2 audits implementation. We prepare the pack, brief your interviewees, manage the auditor's requests live and respond to findings with well-structured corrective action. When Stage 2 closes, the certification body makes the decision and the certificate is issued.

Audit pack Interview preparation NC responses Certificate
07Yearly

Surveillance & continual improvement

Cycle

The first year decides whether the certificate stays healthy. We run the annual cycle — surveillance audit with the certification body, internal audit, risk refresh, control re-testing, supplier re-assessment and a new management review — and keep you ready for re-certification in year three.

Surveillance audit Annual risk refresh KRI dashboard Re-certification prep

Can the phases overlap?
Partly. Documentation drafting starts while controls are being configured, and certification body scheduling runs in the background from phase 02. What never overlaps is the risk assessment and the internal audit — an auditor will see through that immediately.
What if we already have some documentation?
Then phase 02 gets shorter. Existing policies, ISO experiences, security baselines and cloud configurations are assessed first — we keep what holds up, rewrite what does not, and only build what is genuinely missing.
Who selects the certification body?
You do, with our recommendation. We compare accreditation, sector experience, audit scheduling and remote/on-site approach, then coordinate directly with their auditor so requests never arrive as a surprise on a Friday afternoon.
What happens if Stage 2 finds a non-conformity?
It is not the end of the road — findings are graded, and most can be closed with a corrective action plan and evidence within the auditor's deadline. We draft the response, collect the evidence and manage the verification so the decision still follows.
