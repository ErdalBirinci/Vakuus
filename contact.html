Good to know

Before you book

Who should join from our side?
One person who can make decisions — a founder, CTO or head of operations. If someone responsible for IT or security can join too, the second half of the conversation gets much more concrete. Two or three people is the sweet spot.
Do we need to prepare anything?
No documents required. If you have the customer email, tender or security questionnaire that triggered this, have it open — it usually answers half the questions by itself.
Is the meeting binding in any way?
No. It is a working conversation that ends with a written recommendation. You decide what happens after that, and you keep the recommendation either way.
We are pre-revenue — is this too early?
If a customer or investor already asks for evidence of security, it is not too early. If nobody has asked yet, the meeting will tell you honestly whether to start now or to wait — and what to prepare in the meantime.
Can the meeting happen on site?
Discovery meetings are normally held by video across Finland. Workshops — scoping, risk assessment, management review — can be held at your premises when the programme calls for it.
