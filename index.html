The reality of certification

Certificates are not won in the audit. They are won in the twelve weeks before it.

Most certification projects stall on the same things: an incomplete risk assessment, evidence that nobody owns, a supplier list that was never reviewed, or an internal audit booked one week before Stage 1. Vakuus exists so that none of that lands on your team.

Before we meet

Questions we hear first

Do we need a certification, or is a self-assessment enough?
If a customer, tender, investor or insurer asks for a certificate, a self-assessment will not close the deal. In the discovery meeting we map the actual requirement first — sometimes the answer is a lighter framework, sometimes a full ISO 27001 audit. We tell you which one you genuinely need.
How long does it take a small company to get certified?
Most small teams reach Stage 2 in roughly three to five months from kickoff, depending on scope, how much documentation already exists and how quickly evidence can be produced. The gap assessment in phase 02 gives you a realistic date rather than a hopeful one.
How much of this lands on our own team?
Less than you expect. We write the documents, run the risk workshop, perform the internal audit and drive the audit preparation. Your team supplies decisions, approvals and access — typically a few hours per phase, never a second job.
Can you work with our existing IT and cloud setup?
Yes. Microsoft 365, Azure, AWS, Google Cloud, Intune, Entra ID, Sentinel, Salesforce and hybrid Windows/Linux estates are the environments we implement controls in every day. We configure what you already run rather than replacing it.
What happens after we get the certificate?
Phase 07. Surveillance audits, annual risk refresh, internal audits, supplier re-assessment and evidence maintenance keep the certificate valid through the three-year cycle. We can run that cycle with you or hand it over to your team with a playbook.
