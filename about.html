Why this exists

An auditor's perspective, sold as advice instead of findings

Most companies meet compliance as a list of findings delivered by a stranger with a deadline. I have been on that side of the table — running surveillance audits, executing enterprise-wide risk assessments and translating regulatory requirements into control objectives that boards can act on.

Vakuus flips the sequence. The controls, the evidence and the documentation are built the way an auditor will later read them, while the work is still easy to change. Nothing is written to pass a review — it is written to be used on the Monday after the certificate arrives.

Independence preserved: the internal audit phase is always performed separately from the implementation work — the same rule certification bodies apply.

Working principles

How engagements actually run

01

Proportionate

Controls scaled to the real risk of a company your size. No 400-page ISMS that nobody maintains.

02

Evidence first

If it is not evidenced, it does not exist. Evidence is captured as controls go live, never rebuilt later.

03

Plain language

Policies and reports written so staff follow them and boards understand them — in English, without jargon.

04

Handover ready

Everything you pay for stays yours: documents, registers, dashboards and a playbook to run it.
